Knowledge Base

How is the Crop Risk Score calculated? expand_more

The Crop Risk Score is a composite metric derived from real-time soil moisture sensors, hyper-local weather forecasts, and historical yield data. It uses a proprietary agronomic model to assess the likelihood of stress events (like drought or pest pressure) within the next 7-14 days. A score above 75 indicates high risk requiring immediate attention.

My soil sensor shows 'Disconnected'. What should I do? expand_more

First, visually inspect the sensor node in the field to ensure it hasn't been physically damaged by machinery or wildlife. Check the battery indicator on the unit if visible. If physically intact, try rebooting the local LoRaWAN gateway. If the issue persists for more than 24 hours, contact support for a replacement unit.

Can I export my field data for agronomist review? expand_more

Yes. Navigate to the 'Field Map' tab, select the specific plot, and click the 'Export Ledger' button in the top right. You can download the historical data in CSV or PDF formats, which are standardized for easy review by certified crop advisors.
